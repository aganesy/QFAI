# 04 Business Rules

## Purpose

- Decompose AC into explicit business rules.
- Every BR must reference one or more AC IDs.
- Every BR names the contract that realizes it in `Contract-Refs`, or `-` when it binds no contract. Contract short IDs resolve through `_policies/05_Contracts.md#Contract Index`.
