No validator emits the violation `TC-0002-0009` expects, and `planner-first` appears nowhere in `packages/qfai/src/**`. Settling that conflict is a Change Request against `AC-0002-0008`, `DR-0002-0001` and `DR-0002-0003`, not a ledger edit.
