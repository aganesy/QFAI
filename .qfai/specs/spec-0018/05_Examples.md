# 05 Examples

## Purpose

- Concretize each business rule into one input and expected pair.
- Every EX references at least one BR through `BR-Ref`.
- Examples `EX-0018-0001` to `EX-0018-0122` are index-aligned: the example whose trailing
  number is _n_ concretizes the rule with the same trailing number. The rows after them carry the rewritten seeds and the
  two rule bundles.
- `Input` is what a test sets up or submits. `Expected` is the observable that falsifies the
  rule when it disagrees.
- A code, reason or cause named here is the one CLI-WF or CLI-WFFILE names. The sets behind a
  closed-set rule stay in the contract. Each member is asserted by its own test case in
  `06_Test-Cases.md`.
- `Notes` names the pack seed an example carries, as `DUS-NNN <perspective>`, and a fault seed
  as `FAULT-NNN`. Seeds in another spec's half are listed in `09_delta.md`
  `## Dropped Pack Seeds`.

## Rewritten seeds

`EX-0018-0123` to `EX-0018-0135`, `EX-0018-0138` to `EX-0018-0140` and `EX-0018-0143` are the seeds whose expected results change, as
DR-0018-0012 and the pack's rewrites state. Their `Expected` cells are the tracked
statement of those rewrites, and the fixtures must match them. The pack is untracked, and the
seed files exist only in an untracked scratch copy. Every other seed is carried into the
fixtures unchanged apart from translation, and its test cases check shape only.

For a routing seed, `Expected` gives the changed fields of the seed's `expected` object and
`repoFacts`. A field it does not name is unchanged. The four seeds that each carry one class
REQ-0007 excludes from `direct` (ROUTE-044, ROUTE-045, ROUTE-022 and ROUTE-024) are rewritten
whole, and their `Expected` gives every field. `routing_create_question` is the
vocabulary token for the one routing-time CREATE question.
