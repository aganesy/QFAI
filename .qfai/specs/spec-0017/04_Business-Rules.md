# 04 Business Rules

## Purpose

- Decompose AC into explicit business rules.
- Every BR must reference one or more AC IDs.
- Every rule below is a property of **QFAI's own toolchain** — `.github/workflows/**`,
  `.github/actions/**`, repository-root `scripts/**`, `packages/qfai/scripts/**`, and the
  test-runner configuration. Nothing here is distributed (DR-0276), so no rule may be read as
  an obligation on `packages/qfai/assets/init/root/.github/workflows/**`.
- Where a rule names the shipped tree, it governs the **lane that scans it**, never the files
  themselves. Those are `spec-0003`'s, per the ownership boundary in
  `.qfai/contracts/cli/shipped-workflows.md`.

## Granularity (required)

- One BR expresses one independently falsifiable rule. Where an AC states a positive
  assertion and the planted-violation form of the same gate, both live in one BR: the negative
  is that rule's oracle, not a second rule. Where the discussion pack gave falsifiability its
  own criterion, it gets its own BR — the reachability rule and the per-rule hygiene fixtures
  are the two cases.
- Sequencing obligations are rules, not notes. Three of them are load-bearing here (verdict
  hardening before any job addition, shipped-tree hygiene coverage after the shipped
  hardening, the shipped-set contract gate at or before the duplicate's deletion), so each
  carries its own BR and can be violated by a merge order rather than by a file's contents.
- Measurement obligations are rules too. A cost or parallelism claim that lands without
  captured before-and-after numbers violates BR-0017-0030 even when the change itself is
  correct (OC-80).

## Reference Column Conventions

- `AC-Refs` and `NFR-Refs` hold comma-separated IDs and nothing else.
- `NFR-Refs` is restricted to the own-CI halves this spec owns, as enumerated in
  `01_Spec.md` § `Applicable NFR`. The shipped-surface halves are not referenced from here.
- `Contract-Refs` is `-` on every row. The one contract this spec is bound by is the shipped
  GitHub Actions workflow set, whose declared identifier is not one of the three traceable
  contract kinds (`CON-API-*`, `CON-DB-*`, `CON-UI-*`), so declaring it in this column would
  be silently untracked. It is cited once, here, instead of per row:
  `.qfai/contracts/cli/shipped-workflows.md` — §6 (the hygiene rules that apply to the shipped
  tree, and the `R-WORKFLOW-HYGIENE-DRIFT` code), §5 (the structural gate and the
  `R-SHIPPED-WORKFLOW-SHAPE-DRIFT` code) and §1 / §3 (the ownership boundary that keeps the
  scanned files out of this spec).
- `Notes` is free prose. IDs written there are not traced by any tool; every obligation
  recorded there is also expressed in the `Rule` cell.
