# CLI-0016: `qfai validate`

## Ownership boundary

This contract holds the rules for `qfai validate`: the profiles it runs, the
findings it raises on the story tree, the contracts and the assistant tree, the
reports it writes, and the line grammar of its text output. It also holds the
drift protocol that its drift checks enforce and the flow scope that
`qfai report --flow` shares with it.

What `qfai report` writes from a validate result belongs to the report
contract, and the ATDD scaffold command belongs to the ATDD-scaffold contract.
The step-tree check belongs to the assistant-steps contract, the routing and
review-profile defaults belong to the assistant-routing contract, and how the
story tree is authored belongs to the story-tree authoring contract.
