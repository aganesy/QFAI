# CLI-0002: Assistant Steps

## Ownership boundary

This contract decides the step tree under `.qfai/assistant/step/`. A step is
one unit of a stage skill's work: what it reads, what it writes, its procedure
and its gate. A parent is a host-visible skill that owns steps, and a common
step is a procedure several parents share. The contract decides the `STEP.md`
frontmatter, the `steps:` list of a parent, how a parent invoked by name runs
its steps, the review each set of steps receives, how steps ship, and the check
that refuses a step tree nothing can use.

`cli-0020-workflow-files.md` decides which steps a plan stage may run and which
of them a plan may mark pass-through. `cli-0015-qfai-workflow.md` decides the
two reviews of a route and the roles that perform them.
`cli-0001-assistant-routing.md` decides the routing entry and review profile of
each step, and `cli-0009-qfai-init.md` the rest of the assistant tree.

## Business rules

| BR-ID | Statement | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-0002-0001 | Steps Live in One Flat Tree - A step is the directory `.qfai/assistant/step/<name>/`, one level under `step/`, whose entry file is `STEP.md`. The directory name equals the `name` in its `STEP.md`, and the name has the form `<owner>-<name>` with no `qfai-` prefix. No file under `step/` is named `SKILL.md`. A step cites a parent's references and templates by project-root-relative path, such as `.qfai/assistant/skill/qfai-sdd/references/sdd-triage.md`, and those files stay under their parent. | EX-0001-0196-21, EX-0001-0210-01, EX-0001-0210-02, EX-0001-0210-03 |
| BR-0002-0002 | The `STEP.md` Frontmatter - A `STEP.md` frontmatter carries `name`, `owner`, `purpose`, `requires`, `roles` and, optionally, `routing-profile`. `owner` is a parent skill or `common`. `purpose` is one sentence naming what the step produces, `requires` is a list, possibly empty, and `routing-profile` names a profile the routing defaults define, which selects the agents that do the step's work. A step's routing profile adds no review. | EX-0001-0210-03, EX-0001-0210-04, EX-0001-0161-07 |
| BR-0002-0003 | Each Stage Skill Lists Its Steps - A skill that owns a step a built-in plan names lists, in its `SKILL.md` frontmatter `steps:`, every step it owns, in the order it runs them when invoked by name; a plan may run them in an order of its own. Any other entry is a `common-*` step. Every step a plan gives one of its stages is on that list. Its frontmatter `requires:` lists every `common-*` step its body runs itself, `common-review-cycle` among them, so that no step it runs is reached only through its body. Its body names each step with the condition that skips it when the skill is invoked by name, and has the agent read only the current step's `STEP.md`; it holds no step's procedure. It carries no `routing-profile:`, and its `roles:` is `orchestrator` plus the union of the `roles:` of the steps it lists. Its entry check and step loop are those of `.qfai/assistant/rule/shared-skill-operating-baseline.md`. It keeps its name and its place among the host's skills. The skill set is read from the owners of the steps the stage-kind vocabulary of `cli-0020-workflow-files.md` lists. | EX-0001-0195-08, EX-0001-0199-03, EX-0001-0200-02, EX-0001-0204-02, EX-0001-0207-04, EX-0001-0208-08, EX-0001-0214-05 |
| BR-0002-0004 | A Step Requires at Most One Hop - A step's `requires`, and a parent's, names only `common-*` steps, and a `common-*` step's `requires` is empty, so no chain of steps is deeper than parent, step and common step. | EX-0001-0210-07, EX-0001-0210-09 |
| BR-0002-0005 | Steps Ship With the Assistant Tree and Stay Out of Host Skill Lists - `qfai init` installs every shipped step under `.qfai/assistant/step/` with the rest of the assistant tree. A plain upgrade leaves a step whose copy differs from the shipped one as it is, and `--force` replaces it, as for a shipped skill. No host skills directory, `.claude/skills/`, `.agents/skills/`, `.codex/skills/` or `.github/skills/`, holds an entry for a step. | EX-0001-0196-01, EX-0001-0196-21, EX-0001-0196-22 |
| BR-0002-0006 | The Step-Tree Check Fails Closed - `qfai validate` reports `QFAI-SKILLS-016` at severity error, naming the file and the cause, for a `SKILL.md` under `step/`; a step directory without `STEP.md`; a `name` that differs from its directory; an `owner` that is neither `common` nor an installed skill that lists steps; a parent `steps:` entry, a plan step or a step's or parent's `requires` entry naming no installed step; a step, not `common-*`, that its owner's `steps:` does not list; a step no parent lists or requires, no plan uses and no step requires, with no exemption for any step; and a step's or parent's `requires` that is not a list or names a step that is not `common-*` or is not installed, or a common step's `requires` that is not empty. The shipped tree raises none of them. `npx qfai workflow plan` refuses a plan that names a step the project lacks with the reason `plan-invalid`, naming the step, and writes nothing. | EX-0001-0210-01, EX-0001-0210-02, EX-0001-0210-03, EX-0001-0210-04, EX-0001-0210-05, EX-0001-0210-06, EX-0001-0210-07, EX-0001-0210-08, EX-0001-0210-09 |
| BR-0002-0007 | Reviews Belong to the Route - Steps carry no review of their own. In a route, the stage its plan marks `review: spec` is followed by the specification review when that stage changed a story-tree or contract file, and the stage marked `review: code` by the code review of the route's whole diff. In a change route that stage is the one running `verify-change-note`, after every step that writes a tracked file but the verify block, so the review reads the final diff; the verify block after it runs the gates, and a fix it makes after the code review is not reviewed again, and its diff is listed in the final report. No other stage is reviewed. A parent skill invoked by name runs one review after its last step: the specification review for `qfai-sdd` and `qfai-discussion`, the code review for a skill that changed code, tests or a change note, and none for `qfai-triage` or for a `qfai-verify` run that wrote nothing. | EX-0001-0225-01, EX-0001-0225-02, EX-0001-0225-03, EX-0001-0225-04, EX-0001-0225-05, EX-0001-0195-10, EX-0001-0195-11 |
| BR-0002-0008 | A Parent Invoked by Name Runs Its Steps in Order - A parent invoked by name runs its listed steps one at a time, in order, skipping only a step whose skip condition its body states and which holds. It reads a step's `STEP.md` only when that step starts, runs no review between steps, and ends at its stage after one review of the steps it ran. | EX-0001-0195-10, EX-0001-0195-11 |
| BR-0002-0009 | The Triage Steps - `qfai-triage` owns nine steps, which close, answer, split or hand over a request and change no tracked project file: `triage-close` states the outcome and the follow-up requests and ends the route; `triage-answer` answers, citing documents, code or diagnosis evidence; `triage-investigate` investigates internals, history and outside facts by reading only; `triage-request-info` asks for the missing information as structured questions and states the deadline for it; `triage-dedupe` finds the same or a successor item, checks that its premise still holds and links it; `triage-decompose` splits the request into child requests with their dependencies; `triage-cluster` groups automated reports by signature and separates environment causes from product causes; `triage-security-intake` takes a vulnerability report privately, sets its severity and holds back publication; and `triage-handoff` gives a person the steps and preconditions of an operation only they can run, and states the result. Each has the `default` routing profile. | EX-0001-0214-05, EX-0001-0214-03, EX-0001-0214-04, EX-0001-0214-08 |
| BR-0002-0010 | The Implement Steps a Route Adds - `qfai-implement` owns sixteen steps beside its first six: `implement-bisect` finds the change that broke the behaviour between a good and a bad revision; `implement-revert` reverts that change; `implement-minimize` minimises the input that reproduces a failure, and no built-in plan runs it; `implement-stress-harness` builds a load or deterministic-scheduling harness that reproduces it; `implement-oracle-parity` builds a differential check against the outside reference; `implement-benchmark` measures a baseline and a result under the same conditions and records the numbers; `implement-refactor` moves structure while the tests stay green, with no new example; `implement-retire` deletes a mechanism's code, tests and documents, and adds no test that it is gone; `implement-sweep` runs a widened check over the whole tree and fixes each new hit or records it in the baseline; `implement-quarantine` quarantines a flaky test and records why; `implement-dep-bump` raises a dependency and checks engines, version floors and API compatibility; `implement-tooling` edits workflows and scripts, records how the change reaches any CI the project ships to others, and records that a release-path check waits for the next release; `implement-backport` checks the backport criteria and moves the change to the release branch; `implement-scaffold` writes, with `qfai atdd scaffold`, the E2E test of each BF and the integration or API test of each AC the flow lacks, each with an empty body; `implement-credentials` sets up per-worker sign-in reuse for acceptance tests that need an authenticated actor; and `implement-acceptance` writes the bodies of empty acceptance tests, keeping each annotation. Each has the `default` routing profile. | EX-0001-0200-02, EX-0001-0215-05, EX-0001-0226-02 |
| BR-0002-0011 | The Verify Steps a Route Adds - `qfai-verify` owns seven steps beside its first three: `verify-change-note` writes the changelog entry, the migration steps and the breaking changes; `verify-repeat-run` shows a test passing on a recorded number of consecutive runs, which is also what lifts a quarantine; `verify-external` asks the reporter, a device or a production-like environment to confirm the fix and records the answer; `verify-manual` follows a written test plan on each environment and records each defect it finds as a follow-up request; `verify-advisory` prepares the advisory, the CVE and the coordinated disclosure; `verify-release-notes` drafts the release notes; and `verify-commit` commits a route's change locally once every gate passed, and runs only inside a route. Each has the `default` routing profile. | EX-0001-0208-08, EX-0001-0214-07 |
| BR-0002-0012 | What the Routes Ask of Existing Steps - `implement-diagnose` reports one verdict from the set `cli-0015-qfai-workflow.md` gives, and in `read-only` mode changes no tracked file. `sdd-triage` decides from the recorded order of precedence which of two disagreeing surfaces owns the truth and states it, decides whether an inert mechanism is wired or retired, reads only the recorded decision in `settled` mode and stops at anything outside it, and at its decision point puts a critical decision to the user and takes any other itself. `implement-tdd` also aligns the surfaces that do not own the truth, shipped assistant text included, moves the tests that pin them, and runs the project's generation or synchronisation command where it has one. `implement-test-fix` passes when its layer holds no defect. | EX-0001-0215-01, EX-0001-0215-02, EX-0001-0215-04, EX-0001-0215-08, EX-0001-0216-05 |
| BR-0002-0013 | Each Pass-Through Step States Its Pass - The `STEP.md` of every step a plan may mark pass-through states what it reads first and what shows it has nothing to write. `sdd-story` passes when the change adds and changes no example because it stays inside the documents that own the truth, the pass naming those documents. `verify-change-note` passes when nothing a user sees changed, or when the project keeps no changelog. `maintain-edit` passes when no document the change makes wrong exists. | EX-0001-0216-07, EX-0001-0216-08 |
