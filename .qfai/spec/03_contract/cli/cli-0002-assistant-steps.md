# CLI-0002: Assistant Steps

## Ownership boundary

This contract decides the step tree under `.qfai/assistant/step/`. A step is
one unit of a stage skill's work: what it reads, what it writes, its procedure
and its gate. A parent is a host-visible skill that owns steps, and a common
step is a procedure several parents share. The contract decides the `STEP.md`
frontmatter, the `steps:` list of a parent, how a parent invoked by name runs
its steps, the review each set of steps receives, how steps ship, and the check
that refuses a step tree nothing can use.

`cli-0022-workflow-files.md` decides which steps a plan stage may run and the
predicates that make a step active. `cli-0017-qfai-workflow.md` decides the
steps and reviewers a work order carries and the independent review the whole
change needs before `finish`. `cli-0001-assistant-routing.md` decides the
routing entry and review profile of each step, and `cli-0011-qfai-init.md` the
rest of the assistant tree.

| BR-0002-0001 | Steps Live in One Flat Tree - A step is the directory `.qfai/assistant/step/<name>/`, one level under `step/`, whose entry file is `STEP.md`. The directory name equals the `name` in its `STEP.md`, and the name has the form `<owner>-<name>` with no `qfai-` prefix. No file under `step/` is named `SKILL.md`. A step cites a parent's references and templates by project-root-relative path, such as `.qfai/assistant/skill/qfai-sdd/references/sdd-triage.md`, and those files stay under their parent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | EX-0001-0203-21, EX-0001-0217-01, EX-0001-0217-02, EX-0001-0217-03                                                                                      |
| BR-0002-0002 | The `STEP.md` Frontmatter - A `STEP.md` frontmatter carries `name`, `owner`, `purpose`, `requires`, `roles` and, for a step with a review of its own, `routing-profile`. `owner` is a parent skill or `common`. `purpose` is one sentence naming what the step produces, `requires` is a list, possibly empty, and `routing-profile` names a profile the review-profile defaults define. A step with no `routing-profile` adds no reviewer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | EX-0001-0217-03, EX-0001-0217-04, EX-0001-0216-06, EX-0001-0167-09                                                                                      |
| BR-0002-0003 | Each Stage Skill Lists Its Steps - A skill that owns a step a built-in plan names lists, in its `SKILL.md` frontmatter `steps:`, every step it owns in the order they run; any other entry is a `common-*` step. Every step a plan gives one of its stages is on that list. Its frontmatter `requires:` lists every `common-*` step its body runs itself, `common-review-cycle` among them, so that no step it runs is reached only through its body. Its body names each step with the condition that skips it and has the agent read only the current step's `STEP.md`; it holds no step's procedure. It carries no `routing-profile:`, and its `roles:` is `orchestrator` plus the union of the `roles:` of the steps it lists. Its entry check and step loop are those of `.qfai/assistant/rule/shared-skill-operating-baseline.md`. It keeps its name and its place among the host's skills. The skill set is read from the owners of the steps the stage-kind vocabulary of `cli-0022-workflow-files.md` lists. | EX-0001-0202-08, EX-0001-0204-02, EX-0001-0206-03, EX-0001-0207-02, EX-0001-0211-02, EX-0001-0214-04, EX-0001-0215-08                                   |
| BR-0002-0004 | A Step Requires at Most One Hop - A step's `requires`, and a parent's, names only `common-*` steps, and a `common-*` step's `requires` is empty, so no chain of steps is deeper than parent, step and common step.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | EX-0001-0217-07, EX-0001-0217-09                                                                                                                        |
| BR-0002-0005 | Steps Ship With the Assistant Tree and Stay Out of Host Skill Lists - `qfai init` installs every shipped step under `.qfai/assistant/step/` with the rest of the assistant tree. A plain upgrade leaves a step whose copy differs from the shipped one as it is, and `--force` replaces it, as for a shipped skill. No host skills directory, `.claude/skills/`, `.agents/skills/`, `.codex/skills/` or `.github/skills/`, holds an entry for a step.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | EX-0001-0203-01, EX-0001-0203-21, EX-0001-0203-22                                                                                                       |
| BR-0002-0006 | The Step-Tree Check Fails Closed - `qfai validate` reports `QFAI-SKILLS-016` at severity error, naming the file and the cause, for a `SKILL.md` under `step/`; a step directory without `STEP.md`; a `name` that differs from its directory; an `owner` that is neither `common` nor an installed skill that lists steps; a parent `steps:` entry, a plan step or a step's or parent's `requires` entry naming no installed step; a step, not `common-*`, that its owner's `steps:` does not list; a step no parent lists or requires, no plan uses and no step requires, with no exemption for any step; and a step's or parent's `requires` that is not a list or names a step that is not `common-*` or is not installed, or a common step's `requires` that is not empty. The shipped tree raises none of them. `npx qfai workflow start` refuses a project missing a step a built-in plan names as fail-closed with cause `contract-undeclared`, and creates no run.                                             | EX-0001-0217-01, EX-0001-0217-02, EX-0001-0217-03, EX-0001-0217-04, EX-0001-0217-05, EX-0001-0217-06, EX-0001-0217-07, EX-0001-0217-08, EX-0001-0217-09 |
| BR-0002-0007 | One Review per Set of Steps - A step's reviewers are the `always_required` reviewers of its `routing-profile`. The steps of one stage of a run, and the steps a parent runs when invoked by name, are reviewed once, after the last of them, by the union of their reviewers, each role once; a parent run by name adds each `conditional_required` reviewer whose condition holds. In a run whose routing result carries `authorization-restored`, a step owned by `qfai-implement` or `qfai-atdd` counts with the `implementation-heavy` reviewers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | EX-0001-0202-10, EX-0001-0202-11, EX-0001-0216-06, EX-0001-0216-07, EX-0001-0216-08                                                                     |
| BR-0002-0008 | A Parent Invoked by Name Runs Its Steps in Order - A parent invoked by name runs its listed steps one at a time, in order, skipping only a step whose skip condition its body states and which holds. It reads a step's `STEP.md` only when that step starts, runs no review between steps, and ends at its stage after one review of the steps it ran.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | EX-0001-0202-10, EX-0001-0202-11                                                                                                                        |
