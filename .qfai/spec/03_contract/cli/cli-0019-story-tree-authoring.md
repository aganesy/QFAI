# CLI-0019: Story-Tree Authoring

## Ownership boundary

This contract decides how `/qfai-sdd` writes the story tree: the layout of the
policy, business-flow, story and contract layers, the order the layers are
written in, where a business rule is declared and what it cites, how IDs are
allocated, how user approvals and open questions are recorded, the
concrete-abstract cycle that checks rules against examples, and what the skill
does as a stage of a route.

`cli-0014-qfai-validate.md` decides the machine checks that read the tree. The
shipped templates and document schemas decide the shape of each file. The
discussion pack stays source material that the discussion contract governs,
and `cli-0015-qfai-workflow.md` decides the routes and the decisions they put
to the user.
