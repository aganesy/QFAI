# Story-Tree Authoring Contract

- Contract scope: `/qfai-sdd` authoring of the policy, flow, story, decision, and contract layers
- Owning specs: `spec-0001` and `spec-0013`
- Used-by: `/qfai-sdd`, migration, and reviewers of story-tree changes

## Ownership boundary

The policy layer gives objectives, principles, terminology, and constraints.
The business-flow layer gives observable behavior as BF, US, AC, and EX. The
contract layer gives executable interface and business-rule obligations. A BR
is defined inside the contract that enforces it, and another contract refers
to that BR ID without restating its statement. The contract rule forms and
the machine checks that read them are defined in `qfai-validate.md`.

`/qfai-sdd` writes concrete behavior before an implementation plan. It
allocates an ID after the highest ID already named in the applicable local
scope, including retired rows. A rule cites at least one complete EX ID. An
EX cites exactly one AC in its own story. A decision or open question is a row
in its respective table. The accepted templates and Markdown schemas define
file shapes; this contract does not duplicate their field-level schema.

The discussion pack remains source material. An SDD decision is recorded in
the story tree. A discrepancy is resolved in the SDD-owned artifact and its
decision evidence, without rewriting the discussion pack as downstream truth.

Each affected flow has `.qfai/evidence/sdd-BF-NNNN.md`. Its `Pre-draft
Grilling` table records the stage checkpoint, participants, frontier,
recommendation, disposition, decision or open-question IDs, and session timing
before the first design write. The shipped `sdd-flow.md` template owns the
column shape; `qfai-validate.md` owns the missing-record finding. The Work
Orders Summary records delegation separately and does not satisfy this
checkpoint.
