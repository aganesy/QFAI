| EX-0001-0132-01 | AC-0001-0132-01 | Given a Japanese-only `proseCritique` of 1200 characters (no whitespace word boundaries), When QFAI-PROT-002 evaluates it, selecting CJK characters as the unit because the text carries CJK, | Then the prose passes: 1200 is under the character cap, and no lower bound applies. An English critique of 350 words passes the same way, measured in words. A critique too short to have been measured in words at all — which is what a whitespace split returns for Japanese prose — passes rather than failing, because the rule is a cap. A 501-word English critique raises `QFAI-PROT-002` naming words, the cap 500 and the count 501; a Japanese critique over 2500 characters raises it naming characters, the cap and the count. |
