# US-0001-0068: E2E Acceptance Test Implementation

## User Story

As a QA engineer, I want one E2E test for each business flow in scope, carrying `QFAI:BF-NNNN`, and every test that drives a browser to locate elements and inject failures through what stays stable when the screens are rebuilt, so that business-flow coverage is traceable and verifiable and browser tests survive a rebuild that keeps the behaviour.
