# US-0001-0296: Check a design for product fit and claims nobody has confirmed

## User Story

As a user whose screens are reviewed against the design anti-pattern list, I want the review to ask whether the screen would still fit another product's name and to put each claim the project's documents do not support to me, so that a design is specific to my product and no claim on my screen is deleted or invented without my say.

## Non-goals

- A detection that flags a screen without a reviewer's judgement.
- Writing new marketing claims for the product.
