# US-0001-0238: Merge conflicted story-tree documents by sentence

## User Story

As a maintainer merging a branch, I want the conflicted story-tree documents merged by table row and by sentence, so that two edits to different sentences of one long row no longer conflict as a whole line.

## Non-goals

- A git merge driver or any change to the table format.
- Merging files outside the story tree, or staging the result.
