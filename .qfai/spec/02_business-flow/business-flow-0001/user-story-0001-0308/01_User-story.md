# US-0001-0308: Write, build and review a marketing page against its own patterns

## User Story

As a project lead whose product has a public site as well as an application, I want a page a visitor sees before signing in to be written, built and reviewed against marketing patterns, so that it is judged by what a marketing page needs and not by application rules or taste.

## Non-goals

- Surface kinds other than `application` and `marketing`.
- A validate finding for a marketing page that lacks a section.
