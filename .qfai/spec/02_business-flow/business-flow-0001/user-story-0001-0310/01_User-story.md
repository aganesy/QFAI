# US-0001-0310: Take interactive widgets from a tested source

## User Story

As an adopter whose agents build screens with a catalogue installed, I want the procurement rule to name the interactive widgets that are never hand-authored and `qfai validate` to warn about a hand-written dialog, menu or tab list and about a listed widget with no accessibility notes, so that a widget's focus, keyboard and dismissal behaviour comes from a tested source or is recorded and tested.
