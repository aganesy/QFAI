# US-0001-0293: Correct a grilling session on its confirmation sheet

## User Story

As a user confirming a grilling session, I want every decision, assumption and open item put to me on one numbered mark-up sheet, so that I correct what was assumed before the work starts.

## Non-goals

- A QFAI command or a shipped page template that builds the sheet.
- A sheet in a delegated session or under a no-question mode.
