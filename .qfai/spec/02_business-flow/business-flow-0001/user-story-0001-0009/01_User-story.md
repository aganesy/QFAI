# US-0001-0009: Traceability chain BF → US → AC → EX ← BR

## User Story

As a maintainer, I want the chain BF → US → AC → EX ← BR defined on the story tree, where each EX cites one AC of its story and each BR, written in the contract that enforces it, cites the EXs it abstracts, so that every example and business rule can be traced to the story it serves.
