# US-0001-0273: Compare first-iteration variants before I choose a direction

## User Story

As a user settling a visual or interaction decision I cannot put into words, I want the prototyping loop's first iteration to show two or three structurally different variants I can switch between on one page, so that I recognise what I prefer by seeing it and then narrow the loop to one prototype.

## Non-goals

- Variants on a later iteration, or a return to an earlier iteration.
- Variants in a restyle or in a run scoped to a confirmed prototype.
- The agent or a review score choosing a variant.
