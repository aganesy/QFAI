# US-0001-0231: Send session feedback to QFAI when the work is complete

## User Story

As an adopter maintainer, I want the agent to review its whole session once every task I gave it is complete, and to ask me whether to file what it found against QFAI, so that the problems QFAI caused reach its maintainers without my having to remember or write them up.

## Non-goals

- A review at a pause in the work, such as a question to me or a phase boundary.
- Filing without my answer, or filing anywhere but the QFAI repository.
- A configuration key that turns the review off.
- Reporting a defect of the adopter's own project.
