# US-0001-0007: Decision and open-question tables

## User Story

As a maintainer, I want every decision and open question of the project recorded on the story tree in two tables, `decisions.md`, whose rows are appended and never edited, and `open-questions.md`, which holds each question while it is open, with triage records, change requests and retired stories included, so that the project keeps one history of its decisions that is never rewritten and parallel branches never conflict over a row's content.

## Non-goals

- The keyword grammar of the rows a validator reads, which the validate contract defines
