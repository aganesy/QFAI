# US-0001-0007: Decision and open-question tables

## User Story

As a maintainer, I want every decision and open question of the project recorded on the story tree in two tables, `decisions.md`, whose rows are appended and keep their IDs, and `open-questions.md`, which holds each question while it is open, with triage records and retired stories included, so that the project keeps one history of its decisions that never loses a row.

## Non-goals

- The keyword grammar of the rows a validator reads, which the validate contract defines
