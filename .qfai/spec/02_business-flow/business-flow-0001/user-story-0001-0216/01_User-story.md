# US-0001-0216: Pass a step that has nothing to write

## User Story

As a user, I want a step that has nothing to write for my change to say why instead of being dropped from the plan, and to do the work when work it owns remains, so that fixed routes stay small for small changes without hiding an obligation.

## Non-goals

- A step that passes without running.
- A pass for a step outside the closed list.
