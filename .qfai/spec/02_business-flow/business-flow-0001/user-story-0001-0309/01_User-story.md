# US-0001-0309: Keep the parts of a business screen out of minimal-scope cuts

## User Story

As a project lead whose agents build business screens, I want the constitution to rank the parts every business screen has above its minimal-scope article, so that navigation, list behaviour, states and feedback are never cut as scope beyond the minimum.

## Non-goals

- A new definition of what satisfies a requirement.
- A change to how a part on the list is built.
