# US-0004-0004: Move the QFAI directories to their singular names

## User Story

As an adopter, I want step 1 to rename every QFAI-owned directory to its singular name and update the configured paths, so that the rest of the migration and the new validators find the project where they look.

## Non-goals

- Renaming a directory the project configured away from its default.
- Renaming host-defined or skill-internal names.
- Deleting anything already at a destination.
