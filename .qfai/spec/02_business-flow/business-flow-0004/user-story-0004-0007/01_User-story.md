# US-0004-0007: Build the flows and stories from the plan

## User Story

As an adopter, I want step 4 to give every story, criterion, example and rule its new ID from the plan and write each into its place in the story tree, so that the judgment the AI made about flows is applied mechanically and can be reviewed.

## Non-goals

- Deciding which flow a story joins.
- Renumbering after a flow is later split or reordered.
