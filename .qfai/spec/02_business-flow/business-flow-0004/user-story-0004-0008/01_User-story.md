# US-0004-0008: Keep every test case as an example

## User Story

As an adopter, I want steps 5 and 6 to turn every test case with no example into an example under the criterion it cites, and to give every example the criterion its test cases point at, so that no case my old spec recorded disappears with `06_Test-Cases`.

## Non-goals

- Guessing a criterion for a case that cites none or several.
