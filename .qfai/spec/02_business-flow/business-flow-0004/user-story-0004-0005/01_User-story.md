# US-0004-0005: Merge every record into the two project tables

## User Story

As an adopter, I want step 2 to turn every decision record, decision log entry, triage record, change request and open question into a row of `decisions.md` or `open-questions.md`, so that no record is lost and each keeps its standing.

## Non-goals

- Rewriting what a record says.
- Judging whether a record still holds.
