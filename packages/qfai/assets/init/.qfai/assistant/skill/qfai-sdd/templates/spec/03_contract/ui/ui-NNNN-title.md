# UI-0003: Order confirmation

## Responsibility

Let a customer confirm an order.

## Inputs

- The order and the customer’s intent to confirm it.

## Outcomes

- The customer can tell whether confirmation succeeded.

## Business rules

| BR-ID           | Statement                                                                | Examples        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| BR-UI-0003-0001 | A customer can distinguish a refused confirmation from a successful one. | EX-0001-0001-01 |
