# Blocking and Advisory Findings

## Blocking

A finding blocks the current example when it demonstrates an unmet acceptance criterion, a failed test or gate, invalid RED or GREEN evidence, stale proof, a missing required reviewer, or a reviewer verdict of REVISE. Record the finding, owner, and next action. Repair the affected artifact and rerun its dependent checks on the new revision.

An upstream contradiction is routed through the drift protocol. Its dependent implementation waits for the approved decision and the upstream owner to update the story or contract.

## Advisory

An advisory identifies a risk that does not change the approved acceptance result. Record its evidence, scope, owner, and follow-up. A reviewer can raise an advisory without manufacturing a blocking requirement. If evidence later shows that the risk violates an approved criterion, reclassify it as blocking and repair it.

A final PASS still requires the blocking reviewers to answer for the current revision. An advisory does not stand in for a verdict.
