# 08 OQ

## Open Questions

### OQ-0001: <decision topic>

- Disposition: deferred
- Gate: require
- Owner: <owner>
- Reason: <why unresolved now>
- Next decision point: <milestone or task trigger>
- Options:
  - Option A: <summary>
  - Option B: <summary>

## Rules

- `Disposition: open` with `Gate: discuss|require|sdd` is blocking.
- If unresolved, use `deferred` and include reason, owner, next decision point, and options.
