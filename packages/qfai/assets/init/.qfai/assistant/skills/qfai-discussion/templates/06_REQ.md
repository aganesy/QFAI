## Priority Legend

- `must`: Required for MVP / first release.
- `should`: Important but deferrable.
- `could`: Nice-to-have.
- `wont`: Explicitly excluded from current scope.

## Rules

- Each REQ must have at least one Source (SRC-ID) reference.
- Status: `draft` → `reviewed` → `approved`.
