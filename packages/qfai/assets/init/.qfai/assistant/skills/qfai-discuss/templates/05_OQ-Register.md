# 05 OQ Register

## OQ Table

| OQ-ID | Title | Gate | Disposition | Owner | Rationale | Options | Next-Decision-Point | Evidence |
| ----- | ----- | ---- | ----------- | ----- | --------- | ------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| OQ-0001 | TBD | discuss | deferred | user | TBD | Option A / Option B (recommended: A) | Trigger and due date | Conversation log |

## Rules

- Allowed `Gate`: `discuss`, `require`, `sdd`.
- Allowed `Disposition`: `open`, `resolved`, `deferred`, `rejected`.
- Before discuss completion, `Disposition: open` must be zero.
- For `deferred` and `rejected`, `Rationale` is mandatory.
- `Options` must include at least two alternatives and one recommended option.
